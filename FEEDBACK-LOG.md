# Owner feedback log (results page design)

Read this before any design work. Add to it every time the owner reacts. Each entry says which screen the comment was about. Do not stretch a comment beyond that screen. Never re-offer something marked DEAD.

## Likes (steer toward these)
- **Numerology chart in blue, black and silver** (black-blue glass balls in chrome rings, silver hex title bars with a dark plate): the colour combination liked best.
- **The first (front) page's depth, shine and polished look.** The owner chose it as best because it was the first thing with depth and shine. They did NOT say they like red.
- **Charts visible** on the results page, with the labelled words beside them (Type, Profile, Authority, Cross; Sun, Moon and planets with signs; the numbers with their one-word labels). The words make it glanceable; the charts are part of it.
- **Three vertical columns, one per system, each with its chart on top and key words below** (the live page's idea) and a lighter page than near-black.
- **Panels are fine.** Only the typical arrangement of them is rejected.
- **Pinnacle and Challenge as two separately labelled columns.**
- **Core numbers in a second column beside Life Path and Maturity** to make the numerology chart more compact.
- **Playing-card idea:** the owner suggested arranging the panels like playing cards. After seeing it: "that is basically what already exists, it's just an overlap."
- **Playing cards, second reaction:** after the spine and triptych pictures: "Did you not read my suggestion??" Then "When did you make them like a fan?" and "That's not what it looks like when people are playing cards and holding them in their hands." Then: "A fan is what I meant. You did not make a fan." So the owner DID mean a fan (a hand of cards held and fanned out). The assistant's earlier picture was not a fan: three cards tilted only 7 degrees, side by side on one baseline, not spreading from a shared point. Do not describe the fan as the assistant's invention or as dead. The fan stays the direction; the next version must be a true fan: cards pivoting from one point at the bottom, spreading in an arc, overlapping, with the front card clear.
- **Show several different options at once** so a direction can be judged. One at a time does not work.

## Dislikes (never do these again)
- **Red.** No red ribbons anywhere. "I don't even like red."
- **Grey, dull grey, dull grey-blue, pale blue wash, "nothing bright".** Never describe any of these as bright. Includes flat grey silver and the pale blue background on the playing-card screen.
- **Heavy all-blue** pages (overwhelming, hard to understand).
- **Dark pages** ("suffocating", "too dark").
- **Stripes** on anything. They look like dark streaks, not shine. A soft glow is not shine.
- **Faded or soft colour and faded text.** Black must be deep black.
- **Bland.** A plain white background was called bland.
- **Looking like every other astrology/numerology/Human Design site or app.** This outranks all else. Structure must differ, not just colour.
- **The current stand-in look:** cream cards, blue borders, three-by-three grid, old fonts. The owner built it as a placeholder.
- **Corner shapes stuck on like stickers; bars and braids as dividers; red ribbons; the solar-system picture on the numerology chart.**
- **Combined or relational reading in the daily/Today features.** Daily features are per person.
- **Redrawing or inventing charts** (my numerology ring of numbers; the Pythagorean square as I drew it).
- **Putting a shortened reading on the results page.**
- **Showing work I can already see fails, or asking whether it is close.**

## DEAD layouts (never offer, never vary)
Plain vertical stack of panels; even grid of tiles; arches and curved cards; swipe pages; tab bars or tabs for the three systems; home screen of large buttons; dial or cabinet menu; sun or other large hero picture; constellation of connected stars; horizontal life-line of bars; beads on wires; the one-system-with-both-people tab layout; the results-as-a-sentence layout; the plain playing-card overlap (too close to the existing page).

## Set decisions
- Numerology chart order: Life Path, Maturity, Internal State (Essence Cycle), Circumstances (Personal Year, Month, Day), Periods (Environment), Pinnacle (Opportunity) and Challenge (Internal Resistance) as two labelled columns, then Expression, Soul Urge, Personality, Balance, Birthday, Attitude, Karmic Lessons, Karmic Debt.
- Tapping a number shows its own definition for that position.
- The results page has to work for two people and leave places for Today, Timeline, Map and a pass offer. Today and Timeline are per person.
- Results page is glanceable chart data; the written reading is separate.
