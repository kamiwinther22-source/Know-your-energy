Privacy

Last updated November 27, 2026

What you give us

  • Name, birth date, and optionally birth time and birth city, for you and for anyone else you enter.
  • A random code from your phone's own unlock (the face, fingerprint or PIN you already use). It applies the free-reading limit and finds your pass. We never see your face, fingerprint or PIN, and we never ask for an email address or a password.

How we use it

Only to write your reading, calculate your charts, limit free readings and keep track of passes. We do not sell it and we do not show ads.

Who receives your details

  • Anthropic, the AI that writes the reading, receives the numbers and chart positions worked out from your details, and your name.
  • Human Design Hub receives birth date, time and place to calculate Human Design charts.
  • Cloudflare runs the site's server, GitHub hosts its pages, and Google Fonts supplies its lettering, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.

What stays on your device

Every reading you run, including day readings, is saved in your browser and stays there until you clear this site's data. The form you filled in is saved until you run another reading. A random unlock code is also kept there so your phone stays unlocked.

What our server keeps

  • The random code for your phone's unlock, kept with no end date. A second random code that keeps you signed in on a browser, kept for 60 days. A one-time random code used while your phone unlocks, kept 5 minutes. A device unlocked with the owner's private key keeps its sign-in code with no end date and is marked as unlimited. A note that your free reading was used, kept with no end date. Your pass type and end date. We keep these so a free reading cannot be used twice and your pass can be found. None of it holds a name or email.
  • For day readings, tied only to a scrambled form of your unlock code: for a free reading, which day it was for (kept up to 800 days). A reading that is being written is also marked for a few minutes so it cannot be started twice.
  • Finished day readings are not kept. Once a day reading is delivered, our server holds no copy of it. Copies saved before November 27, 2026 are removed automatically by December 11, 2026.
  • A scrambled form of your connection address, kept for up to two days, only to limit how many new phones one connection can add in a day, and how many wrong owner-key tries one connection can make.
  • A temporary copy of a reading while it is delivered, with the chart details used to write it. It is removed automatically after one hour.
  • A chart cache keyed by birth date and time only, and a cache of city names and time zones. Neither is tied to a name or email, and neither has an end date.
  • Anonymous counts of visits, button taps, and readings written with their cost (the detailed cost list is kept 90 days). A count of free readings for each day, kept 3 days. These cannot be traced to you.
  • Cloudflare, which runs the server, also keeps its own request logs for up to 7 days: the web address requested (for a city search, the letters typed), the time, the result, and the request details your browser sends, which can include your connection address and browser type. Any error messages the site's own code writes are kept there too.

Payments

Passes are not on sale yet. When they are, payment will be taken on the payment processor's own secure page, and we will not see or store your card details.

Children

The site is for adults. A parent or guardian may enter a child's name and birth date to get a parent and child reading, and we use those details only to write that reading.

What we know about you

Nothing that names you. About your phone, we keep whether its free reading has been used, whether a pass is active, and the day readings listed above. All of it is tied only to a random code. We do not track you across other sites.

Exposure of information

If we learn that personal information we hold has been exposed, we will tell the people affected and any authority the law requires.

Changes

We may update this page, and the date above shows the latest version.
