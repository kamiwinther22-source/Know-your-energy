Know Your Energy

Privacy

Last updated November 17, 2026

What you give us

  • Name, birth date, and optionally birth time and birth city, for you and for anyone else you enter.
  • Your email address, which applies the free-reading limit and finds your pass.

How we use it

Only to write your reading, calculate your charts, limit free readings and keep track of passes. We do not sell it and we do not show ads.

Who receives your details

  • Anthropic, the AI that writes the reading, receives the numbers and chart positions worked out from your details, and your name.
  • Human Design Hub receives birth date, time and place to calculate Human Design charts.
  • Cloudflare runs the site's server, GitHub hosts its pages, and Google Fonts supplies its lettering, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.

What stays on your device

Your latest reading, the form you filled in and your email are saved in your browser. "Forget my details" on the results page removes them.

What our server keeps

  • Your email, a note that your free reading was used, and your pass type and end date, until we delete them or you ask us to.
  • A temporary copy of a reading while it is delivered, removed automatically after one hour.
  • A chart cache keyed by birth date and time only, and a cache of city names and time zones. Neither holds a name or email.
  • Anonymous counts of visits, button taps, and readings written with their cost. These cannot be traced to you.

Payments

Passes are not on sale yet. When they are, payment will be taken on the payment processor's own secure page, and we will not see or store your card details.

Children

The site is for adults. A parent or guardian may enter a child's name and birth date to get a parent and child reading, and we use those details only to write that reading.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about your email address, or ask us to correct or delete it. Write to Know-your-energy@proton.me from that address. We do not track you across other sites.

Exposure of information

If we learn that personal information we hold has been exposed, we will tell the people affected and any authority the law requires.

Changes

We may update this page, and the date above shows the latest version.
