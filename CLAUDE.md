# Read this first

## NO GREY. This is a rule, not a taste note.
The owner does not want a grey website and has said so many times. Never use flat grey as a main, background or large-area colour anywhere: not for a page, a map, a panel, a frame or a block of text. Grey is not silver: silver is metallic, with real light and depth, and is fine. Flat grey is not.
Before showing the owner ANY picture, run `python3 tools/test/grey_check.py <pictures>`. If it says FAIL, redo the work before showing it. Do not explain a grey result, fix it.

**Before doing anything, read `HANDOFF.md` (one page).** It has the owner's working rules, what is live, what is decided and what is open.

The most important rule: when the owner says "do it", "make it live", "push it" or "go", do it and put it live immediately. Do not ask again.

Other files in this repo (`FEEDBACK-LOG.md`, `HANDOFF-NOT-LIVE.md`, `DESIGN-CONCEPTS.md`) are old notes. They contain absolute rules and context-specific statements that the owner says blocked progress. Do not read them unless the owner asks.
