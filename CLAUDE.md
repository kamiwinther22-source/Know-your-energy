# Know Your Energy: working notes

## How to work with the owner (this comes first)
- **The design goal that outranks everything else: the site must NOT look like every other astrology, numerology or Human Design site or app.** Structure and layout have to be different, not only colour or finish. Every design choice is judged against this first. Working out what "different" means is the assistant's job.
- **Show real options and let the owner choose.** The owner does not know what the possibilities are. Show several genuinely different, finished-looking options (screenshots of the real page with real data, never rough sketches or stand-ins), then let them pick. Do not narrow to one option for them and do not hand back a menu of small variations.
- **When the owner says "this isn't it", that option is dead.** Never bring it back or offer small variations of it unless they specifically ask. Treat it as the wrong answer and look for something different.
- **Do not work ahead of the owner.** They review slowly and comment on older things. If they say wait, wait. Do not build new versions while they are still commenting on earlier ones; answer what they are commenting on.
- **Keep every piece of feedback attached to the screen it was about.** A comment applies only to what was on screen when it was made. Do not stretch it to other screens or to the whole site. Use the accumulated feedback to move steadily toward what the owner wants: each new round must be a deliberate step from what they reacted to, never a restart from scratch and never a random guess.
- **Do not put words in the owner's mouth or overgeneralize.** Do not turn a dislike of one thing into a ban on a whole category (for example, dislike of red or grey does not mean no colour). Colour is undecided; blue, silver and black is the combination they liked best, and a heavy all-blue page is too much.
- **Layouts the owner has ruled out for the results page (never offer these or variations of them):** a vertical stack of panels; a grid of tiles or cards, including overlapping cards and arches; a bottom tab bar or tabs for the three systems; swipe pages; a home screen of large buttons; a dial or cabinet used as a menu; a sun or other large decorative hero picture; a constellation of connected stars; a horizontal life-line of bars; beads on wires. The all-blue colour treatment on the last three was also too heavy.
- **No stripes, on anything.** No hard-banded gradients, diagonal sheens, repeating lines or striped fills, on backgrounds, frames, bars or text. Shine must come from smooth highlights and depth, not bands. The owner's reason: stripes do not read as shine, they only produce dark streaks. Shine means brightness and glow, not dark and light bands.
- **The results page is glanceable chart data, not a shortened reading.** The written reading is its own thing. Do not put a condensed or prose version of the reading on the results page: it is shallow, not glanceable, and makes people re-read what the reading already says. Ruled out for that reason: the "results written as a sentence" layout. The framed certificate and magazine cover were NOT judged (the owner could not look at them because of stripes); they are not ruled out.
- **Known set decisions:** the numerology chart order is Life Path, Maturity, Internal State (Essence Cycle), Circumstances (Personal Year, Month, Day), Periods (Environment), Pinnacle (Opportunity) and Challenge (Internal Resistance) as two separately labelled columns, then Expression, Soul Urge, Personality, Balance, Birthday, Attitude, Karmic Lessons, Karmic Debt. Each number tapped shows its own definition. No vertical stack results page, no headline picture or hero decoration on the results page, no combined or relational reading in the daily features, and daily features are per person.
- **What the owner says in the current message is the instruction.** It overrides anything written in this file, in earlier chats, in commit messages or in code comments. If they change their mind, follow the new words. Never quote an old rule back at them as a reason to push back.
- Do exactly what they say. Don't add, don't subtract, don't reinterpret. If a request could mean two things, ask one short question before spending effort.
- Design is reviewed as **pictures at phone width (about 390 px), whole pages, not strips**. For design work, show a picture and wait for approval before changing the site or writing real wording.
- Say plainly what is done and what isn't. Don't oversell.
- Their usage is limited. Don't tweak or redo something they rejected, and don't build ahead of a decision.
- When the owner specifically asks for a change, merge it to `main` (that publishes the site). Don't merge anything they didn't ask for. Experiments go on a separate branch.
- **There are no standing look rules.** The look of the front page and of the results page is undecided and is whatever the owner says now. Metallic styling is not wanted.

## What the site is
- Static front end on GitHub Pages (`index.html`, `red-look.css`, `CNAME` = know-your-energy.com) plus a Cloudflare Worker (`worker.js`, KV namespace `PASSES`). A merge to `main` deploys both (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`, which also builds the city list from GeoNames).
- Charts: numerology (`numerology-calculator.js`), astrology (`astro-engine.js` with `circular-natal-horoscope-js`, city lookup in `cities.js`), Human Design (a paid API called from the Worker). The written reading comes from the Claude API; cost is about $0.13 per reading (see the Worker's `/usage` page).
- Promises to visitors: birth details and readings are not kept on the server, no emails are sent to them, and an email address is their pass (unverified).
- `PAYMENTS_ENABLED` is `false` (no payment processor yet). Three family emails are unlimited.
- Testing is done with Playwright in Chromium against stubbed Worker calls. It cannot test real iPad/Safari, the Cloudflare dashboard, payments or real reports.
