# Look upgrade: decisions so far (owner's words, this round only)

Each line is attached to what was on screen when it was said. Update this file every time the owner reacts.

## Settled (do not revisit)
- Keep the layout and positions exactly as they are. Change only colour, material and light.
- The solar system stays where it is, on purpose. Pictures show only the 390 px screen: nothing past the right margin.
- No red site (red on a small object is fine). No single colour theme. No whole page in one colour: it needs detail, but no background pattern and no hard-line separations between parts.
- Nothing blurry: no frosted glass, soft glows or feathered shadows. Every edge crisp.
- Gloss is required. No flat grey. Surfaces curved, not flat sheets.
- Reflections only from things on the page (sphere, buttons). No invented objects.
- No metal. Buttons are real glossy objects, not flat stickers.
- Not teal. Not Christmas (no red with green). Not black-and-white / newspaper. Not country club.
- Wanted: classy, elegant, sleek. Fine jewelry or precious stone: diamonds, not plastic.
- Different from the usual wellness / astrology / numerology sites.
- Show only checked, honestly better work. Do not act without agreement. Do not read the old notes.

## Dead
- Anything dark / night-sky (the dark opal version): the owner hates the darkness.
- Brighter red or coral-red page as a replacement for the old red (the coral-pink diamond page is at risk of this).
- Citron / lemon (too loud), racing green + vermilion, black and ivory, white and clear.
- Hard-line regions as separate pieces of the page.

- Pearl, and anything pink, purple or pastel: it read like a kid's toy.

## The feel (owner's words)
- The feel of the whole site is fine jewelry, diamonds, the expensive feel of diamonds. Not cheap, not plastic.

## Later decisions (owner's words)
- Scope: work ONLY on the background, the buttons and where people enter information. The solar system is fine as it is; do not spend time on it.
- Pearl is wanted, and a pearl is creamy white. Not pink, not purple. Pearl was never ruled out by the owner; it was dropped by mistake.
- No blue. The owner reads the pale blue as grey.
- The page must feel like a substance you could touch, lit by real reflecting light, not a flat surface with drawn marks. Use real skills, not shortcuts.
- Light comes from the sun in the solar system (owner's idea). Never default to the top-left corner.

- Colour feedback on the coral/pink diamond page and the cream pearl page: not appealing, flat and dull, possibly too much orange. The owner cannot name what is wrong beyond that; the way colour, shading and light were applied reads cheap and low effort.
- Diamonds, glamour, shine, sparkle: real light must visibly pass through the objects. Dark speckles/grit on a surface is the opposite of shine. Nothing may look like a clown party. It should feel fragile, expensive and make you want to touch it.
- Agreed next step: a one-button, one-entry-field test proving light bends through glass (real refraction), before building anything else.

## Where things stand
- Pearl preview: dead (pink/purple, kid's toy).
- Diamond preview: failed (reads as pale pink plastic; needs real refraction).
- Opal preview: dead (dark, heat-map flashes, teal rings).
- Previews are made from a scratch copy of the site outside the repo; the live site and `main` are untouched.
- Pearl real-render preview (3D heightfield, sun as the point light): creamy and bright with real depth, but the shaded edges come out as heavy brown outlines and the buttons show no convincing lustre. Not good enough to show yet.
