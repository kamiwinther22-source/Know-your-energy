# Look upgrade: decisions so far (owner's words, this round only)

Each line is attached to what was on screen when it was said. Update this file every time the owner reacts.

## Settled (do not revisit)
- Keep the layout and positions exactly as they are. Change only colour, material and light.
- The solar system stays where it is, on purpose. Pictures show only the 390 px screen: nothing past the right margin.
- No red site (red on a small object is fine). No single colour theme. No whole page in one colour: it needs detail, but no background pattern and no hard-line separations between parts.
- Nothing blurry: no frosted glass, soft glows or feathered shadows. Every edge crisp.
- Gloss is required. No flat grey. Surfaces curved, not flat sheets.
- Reflections only from things on the page (sphere, buttons). No invented objects.
- No metal. Buttons are real glossy objects, not flat stickers.
- Not teal. Not Christmas (no red with green). Not black-and-white / newspaper. Not country club.
- Wanted: classy, elegant, sleek. Fine jewelry or precious stone: diamonds, not plastic.
- Different from the usual wellness / astrology / numerology sites.
- Show only checked, honestly better work. Do not act without agreement. Do not read the old notes.

## Dead
- Anything dark / night-sky (the dark opal version): the owner hates the darkness.
- Brighter red or coral-red page as a replacement for the old red (the coral-pink diamond page is at risk of this).
- Citron / lemon (too loud), racing green + vermilion, black and ivory, white and clear.
- Hard-line regions as separate pieces of the page.

- Pearl, and anything pink, purple or pastel: it read like a kid's toy.

## The feel (owner's words)
- The feel of the whole site is fine jewelry, diamonds, the expensive feel of diamonds. Not cheap, not plastic.
