# Quality checklist (must be complete before stopping or showing the owner anything)

Every item is checked against the actual picture at 390 px and a close-up crop. Each item is marked PASS or FAIL, and the marked list is shown to the owner with the picture. A FAIL is fixed before showing, or reported plainly as a FAIL. "Not broken" is not done.

## A. Before building
1. Written target: the material of every part, the colour of every part, what is carved and what is raised.
2. The proper method for each part is named. Anything that cannot be done properly with the tools here is told to the owner before starting, not after.
3. The real page content and layout are used (real words, real data), unless the owner asked otherwise.

## B. Build order
1. Form: shape of the surface, what is cut in, what rises out, edge profiles.
2. Material: colour (for metal this is the colour of its reflections), finish (polished, satin), surface detail. Judged under neutral light first.
3. Light: what the surfaces reflect (bright areas and dark areas placed on purpose), plus the light that casts shadows.
4. Check in place on the whole page, then adjust exposure only slightly. A big brightness change means the light or the colour is wrong; fix that instead.

## C. Before showing
- Colour: no part reads grey unless the owner asked for grey. Colours are full, not faded. Not the dull grey-blue (#e4eeff to #cfe0ff). No deep jewel shades, no soft glowing cool gradients, no tan or cream.
- Material: a stranger would name the material without being told. Metal shows bright highlights and dark reflections; edges catch light; polished and satin areas differ. Porcelain is white and its gloss is visible.
- Unity: buttons, fields and lettering are made of, cut into or raised from the material. Nothing looks like a sticker on top.
- Depth: cut things have walls and shadow; raised things cast shadow.
- No stripes, bands or streaks. No glow called shine. No filters or overlays faking a material.
- Text: every word readable at 390 px; nothing cut off; spacing correct.
- Not like other astrology, numerology or Human Design sites.
- Compared with the written target in A.1, not with the last failure.

## D. The owner's comments (this list grows; every new version is checked against all of it)
Each time the owner says what they don't like, it is added here as a requirement and carried into every later version, not only the next one. A new version that repeats any earlier complaint is a FAIL.
