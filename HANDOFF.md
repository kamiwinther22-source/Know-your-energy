# Know Your Energy: handoff (short on purpose)

The owner does not code. Do not explain how you did things. Say what happened and what you need from them.

## WORKING RULES (the owner's words, in order of importance)
1. **"Do it", "make it live", "push it", "go" or "yes I want that" means: do it and put it live NOW.** Merge to `main`, check the live site, send a picture. Never ask again. The owner said "do it" three times for the unlock change and was still asked. This is the most important rule.
2. **Answer the owner's message first**, in a few words, before doing anything else. They are often watching and interrupt when they cannot tell what is happening.
3. **Do exactly what is said.** Do not add, subtract or widen it. A rule applies only to what it was said about, unless the owner says it applies everywhere.
4. **A statement is not an order.** "I hate the grey base" is feedback. Do not build in response to feedback. Wait for a direction, or for a direct question such as "can you fix it?".
5. **If the owner asks whether something is right and it is not, fix it, show it, and wait for "go".** If they have not said go, show before live. If they have said go, do not ask.
6. **Always show the whole page**, not crops. For the form, show Relational mode (Person 1 and Person 2).
7. **Phone first.** Desktop and tablet are untouched and are for a later thread. Everything phone-specific is in the last block of `red-look.css` and in small scripts in `index.html`.
8. **Small tasks stay small.** "Move the button up" is a small change. Do not rebuild layouts.
9. **Short messages.** No step-by-step "how". The owner will not read it.
10. **Do not spend credits on testing hundreds of sizes.** About five phone sizes is enough: 320x568, 375x629, 390x664, 412x770, 430x739.
