# Owner's design references (24 Oct)

Photos of an iPad screen, taken by the owner as examples of "higher quality design" whose TECHNIQUES they want for accents, centrepieces or the background theme. The fine pattern lines in the photos are only the phone camera on the iPad; the owner sees crisp images. Ignore the unrelated page chrome around the images (ads, other pins).

1. `1-ink-cat.jpg`: ink-wash cat. Deep black calligraphic strokes that taper, translucent smoky veils overlapping, fine whisker and wisp lines, soft pearl-grey ground, lots of empty space, one S-curve.
2. `2-white-rose-silk.jpg`: white rose in folds of translucent silk. Near-monochrome pearl and white, layers of see-through fabric, light passing through, a faint blush only at the heart of the rose, a spiral composition.
3. `3-glass-heart.jpg`: clear glass heart on a grey studio floor. Real refraction, crisp window reflections on the thick rim, bright specular edges, a cast shadow grounding it.
4. `4-silver-violinist.jpg`: luminous silver line-art violinist on a periwinkle-to-lavender gradient. Fine flowing white lines, polished silver shading on the violin, light on every edge.

Common techniques: light is the subject (specular edge light, rim light); translucent layers (smoke, silk, glass) instead of opaque panels; flowing S-curves and spirals; near-monochrome light grounds with one soft accent; crisp bright edges against soft gradients; fine line detail as accents; cast or contact shadows; generous empty space. None of them uses a starfield, a cosmic glow or a deep jewel ground.
